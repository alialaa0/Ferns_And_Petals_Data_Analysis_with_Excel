# Ferns & Petals Sales Analysis | Excel

An end-to-end **sales data analysis and business intelligence project using Microsoft Excel**.  
The project analyzes Ferns & Petals (FNP) e-commerce data to identify sales trends, product performance, customer behavior, geographic demand, and delivery performance.

## 📌 Project Overview

Ferns & Petals (FNP) is an e-commerce business specializing in gifts for different occasions, including:

- Diwali
- Raksha Bandhan
- Holi
- Valentine's Day
- Birthdays
- Anniversaries

The dataset contains information about **orders, customers, products, prices, quantities, dates, cities, and occasions**.

The objective of this project is to transform the raw transactional data into a structured analytical model and create a dashboard that helps answer important business questions related to:

- Sales performance
- Product performance
- Customer spending
- Geographic demand
- Delivery efficiency
- Occasion-based sales

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis was designed around the following business requirements:

### 1. Overall Revenue
Determine the total revenue generated from all orders.

### 2. Order & Delivery Performance
Calculate the average order and delivery time to evaluate operational performance.

### 3. Monthly Sales Performance
Analyze revenue across the months of 2023 to identify sales trends and seasonal patterns.

### 4. Top Products by Revenue
Identify the products generating the highest revenue and determine the strongest product categories.

### 5. Customer Spending
Analyze customer spending behavior and calculate the average amount spent per customer/order.

### 6. Top 5 Product Performance
Track the sales performance of the five highest-revenue products.

### 7. Top 10 Cities by Orders
Identify the cities generating the highest number of orders to understand geographic demand.

### 8. Order Quantity vs. Delivery Time
Analyze whether larger orders are associated with longer delivery times.

### 9. Revenue by Occasion
Compare revenue across different occasions to identify the most valuable sales opportunities.

### 10. Product Popularity by Occasion
Determine which products perform best for specific occasions to support targeted marketing and product planning.

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses three main datasets:

### Orders

Contains transaction-level information such as:

- Order ID
- Customer ID
- Product ID
- Order Date
- Delivery Date
- Quantity
- Occasion

### Customers

Contains customer and geographic information:

- Customer ID
- Customer details
- City

### Products

Contains product information:

- Product ID
- Product Name
- Product Category
- Price

The tables are connected using their respective keys to create a relational data model for analysis.
